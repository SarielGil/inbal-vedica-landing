FAQ

How should I choose an Ayurvedic practitioner in Tel Aviv?

There is no objective single “best” practitioner for everyone. Compare training, experience with your concern, how the process handles medications, blood tests and diagnoses, language, process transparency, public third-party reviews and personal fit. VEDICA publishes its process, practitioner background, Tel Aviv address and Infomed profile so these points can be checked.
