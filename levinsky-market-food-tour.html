Herbs, memory and the journeys between them

With Inbal, a bridge between worlds

The warmth of cinnamon, the scent of dried rosebuds, a familiar spice blend: in Levinsky, everyday ingredients can open a doorway to a distant place. With Inbal, a local, discover the family memories and journeys carried into the market by the people who made it their home.

Her knowledge of Ayurveda and her Persian-Iranian heritage connect the stalls around you with a wider story of exchange. Through stories of the Silk Roads and the region's spice and incense routes, explore how ingredients and ideas traveled between India, Persia and the Mediterranean.

In today's Levinsky, that wider story becomes personal. Hear how Greek, Persian, Turkish and Balkan immigrants brought beloved flavors and traditions to a new home in Tel Aviv. Inbal brings the ancient exchanges and the market's living cultures into conversation, with stories, questions and practical ideas to take into your own kitchen.

FAQ

Can I join the tour on my own?

Yes. Book an individual place on the shared tour through GetYourGuide, or choose the private option for your group. Current availability, prices and offers appear there after you select a date. Contact Inbal on WhatsApp for a custom group quote.

What makes this Levinsky Market tour different?

Inbal, a local, brings Ayurveda and her Persian-Iranian heritage together as a cultural bridge. Explore herbs, spices and migration stories in Levinsky Market, with tastings, an Ayurvedic infusion and a starter herb and spice kit to continue exploring at home.

Can I book a private Levinsky Market Ayurveda and spice tour for a corporate team?

Yes. The tour can be planned as a private or corporate Levinsky Market Ayurveda and spice tour for teams, offsites, client hosting and international groups. The route is adapted to the date, group size, preferred language, dietary needs and requested experience.

Is the Levinsky Market tour available in English?

Yes. The tour can be held in English or Hebrew and is suitable for English-speaking groups, native English-speaking visitors, international guests, corporate teams, private groups and visitors to Tel Aviv.

Is Levinski Market the same place as Levinsky Market?

Yes. Levinsky Market is sometimes searched online as Levinski Market. This tour explores the same spice market area through herbs, Ayurveda and cultural stories, with language and route adapted to the group.

Is this a food tour or a herbal medicine tour?

This is a market tasting tour with herbs, spices and cultural stories, guided by Inbal through Ayurveda and her Persian-Iranian heritage. Tastings, snacks and an infusion are included; a full lunch is not included. The tour is an educational experience and does not provide personal medical advice.

Who is the tour for?

Solo visitors, couples and friends can join a shared tour. Private and custom group tours suit senior groups, corporate teams, offsites and international guests. Guidance is available in English or Hebrew.

Can dietary needs be considered?

Yes. Share food sensitivities, allergies, kosher needs or other dietary considerations before booking so the route and tastings can be planned responsibly where possible.

What is included in the Levinsky Market tour?

The experience includes English or Hebrew guidance through Levinsky Market, an Ayurvedic infusion, snacks, market tastings and a starter herb and spice kit. Additional stops and tastings vary with the group, season, route and vendor availability.

How are private and group tours planned?

Each tour is planned individually according to the preferred date, group details, language and dietary needs. Optional lunch, additional tastings and other treats can be discussed and included in the tailored proposal.

How long is the tour and where does it start?

The tour usually takes about 2–2.5 hours and starts at 9:30 AM, Sunday–Friday, subject to availability. Meet at Aluf Batslut Alley (סמטת אלוף בצלות), Tel Aviv-Yafo. Alternative private start times can be arranged in advance, subject to availability.

Can Inbal help us explore after the tour?

Yes. Before you say goodbye, Inbal can suggest places to find lunch and help you get your bearings for exploring the shops and neighborhood at your own pace. Lunch and any purchases after the tour are paid separately.

Is the tour medical advice?

No. The tour is an educational experience focused on herbs, Ayurveda and culture. It discusses traditional uses of herbs, spices and infusions in a grounded way, but it does not replace personal medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
