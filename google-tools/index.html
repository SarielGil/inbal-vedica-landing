inbal_web
VEDICA Google account tools

inbal_web connects VEDICA’s authorized operators to its Google Ads, Google Search Console and Google Analytics data for reporting, marketing analysis and account changes requested by the business.

This is the application information page for the name inbal_web shown on the Google authorization screen. VEDICA is the Ayurveda, workshop and market-tour business of Inbal Hanasab in Tel Aviv.

Access is limited to authorized VEDICA operators. There is no public registration. Google authorization is started from the operator’s local tools, and an operator can access only accounts and properties for which they have permission.

Reports and analysis

Review campaign performance, advertising costs, search terms, website search visibility, aggregate website activity and contact-click events. Compare periods and prepare recommendations or advertising drafts.

Authorized account actions

When requested by the business, use the official Google APIs to configure advertising campaigns or submit a website sitemap. The connected Google Ads MCP exposes reporting tools; authorized changes use a separate API workflow.

How Google access is used

Google permissions are used to retrieve the selected business data or perform a requested account action. Credentials are kept in the operator’s local configuration, outside the public website. Selected reporting results may be processed by an AI assistant to produce the operator’s requested analysis.

The inbal_web privacy policy describes the data accessed, its use, storage and sharing, and how access can be revoked.
