Application privacy policy

Privacy for inbal_web

This policy describes VEDICA’s private Google reporting and account-management integration, inbal_web. It applies to Google account data accessed through these tools and to the reports they produce.

Operator: Inbal Hanasab, VEDICA, Tel Aviv, Israel. Contact: inbal.ph@gmail.com. Effective date: 8 November 2026.

Who can use the integration

The tools are used by VEDICA’s authorized business operators. They are not a public sign-up service. A Google account holder grants access through Google’s authorization process, or a property owner grants an authorized service account access. The tools can access only the accounts and properties covered by those permissions.

Google data accessed

  • Google Ads: business account identifiers, campaign and ad configuration, keywords, reported search terms, budgets, costs, impressions, clicks, conversion-action settings and reported conversions. Account changes are made only as requested by an authorized operator.
  • Search Console: verified property information, search queries and pages, clicks, impressions, position, indexing inspection results and sitemap information.
  • Google Analytics: property information and aggregate website reports, such as sessions, acquisition sources, landing pages, approximate geography, device or language groups and contact-related event counts.
  • Authorization and setup: OAuth credentials, granted scopes and access or refresh tokens needed to maintain authorized access. Google Cloud permissions may also be used to set up or check APIs for this integration.

The integration does not request access to Gmail messages, Drive documents, contacts or calendars. It is not intended to retrieve patient records or consultation notes. Operators should not place sensitive personal or health information in reporting requests.

How the data is used

Data is used to answer the operator’s reporting questions, compare marketing performance, check website visibility, assess contact-intent measurements, prepare advertising drafts and carry out authorized account configuration. A contact-click event is treated as an intent signal, not proof of a completed booking.

Google account data is not sold, used for data-broker services or used by this application to develop generalized AI models. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Processing and sharing

Google processes API requests to provide the connected services. VEDICA’s authorized operators can review retrieved data and saved reports. Selected query results and reporting prompts may be sent to OpenAI through the operator’s AI assistant to generate the requested analysis, explanations or drafts. Processing and retention by that service depend on the product and account data controls in use; see OpenAI’s data controls documentation.

Reports may be shared with VEDICA’s advisers or published when an authorized operator explicitly requests it. Google credentials and tokens are not intended for publication or inclusion in AI reporting prompts. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

Storage, access and retention

OAuth and service-account credential files are stored in the operator’s local configuration with restricted file access, outside the public website source. Reporting exports and audit notes may be saved in the operator’s workspace. Those reports are retained while needed for business reporting and audit work; the tools do not currently enforce an automatic expiry for saved exports.

AI conversation or provider-side retention is governed separately by the service and account controls used by the operator. This application does not promise a shorter provider-side retention period or automatic deletion from those services.

Revoking access and requesting deletion

An account holder can review and revoke the application’s authorization in Google Account third-party connections. A property owner can also remove a service account’s access in the relevant Google product. Revocation stops continued authorized access but does not by itself delete reports that were already exported.

To request removal of locally stored credentials or reports, or to ask what has been retained, email inbal.ph@gmail.com with “inbal_web privacy” in the subject. Requests are handled by the authorized operator; information needed to verify the request may be required. Data retained by an AI provider must also be managed using that provider’s account and deletion controls.

These public information pages

The application information and privacy pages do not start an OAuth flow, collect Google passwords, include analytics scripts or offer a data-entry form. The website’s hosting provider may process standard web-request metadata to deliver the pages. This policy describes the integration; it does not replace information about other services or booking providers linked from the VEDICA website.

Changes and questions

Updates are published here with a revised effective date. Questions about the application or this policy can be sent to inbal.ph@gmail.com.
